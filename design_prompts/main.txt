Design a premium mobile music-practice app UI called “ChordFlow” for iPhone and Android.

The app helps musicians create, edit, practice, and export songs with lyrics, chords, guitar tabs, piano notes, and instrumental arrangements.

Show a polished multi-screen mobile product presentation with these screens:
1. Library screen with saved songs, search, and filters
2. Create Song screen with a clear first choice between “With lyrics” and “Instrumental”
3. Song Viewer screen with switchable views: Lyrics, Lyrics + Chords, Guitar, Piano, Notes, Practice
4. Display/filter bottom sheet with toggles for lyrics, chords, tabs, piano notes, diagrams, timestamps, and section labels
5. Song Editor screen with editable lyrics and chord placement above the words
6. Practice screen with auto-scrolling lyrics and chords, speed slider, start/pause controls, and current line highlighting
7. Guitar chord popup in a bottom sheet with a clean fretboard diagram
8. Piano chord popup in a bottom sheet with highlighted keyboard notes
9. PDF export screen that exports according to the currently selected view

Visual direction:
- premium, elegant, calm, musician-focused
- dark mode primary presentation
- subtle light mode secondary accents
- clean editorial typography
- strong visual hierarchy
- soft shadows, refined spacing, smooth sheet overlays
- high readability during practice mode
- no generic neon SaaS look
- no purple-blue AI gradients
- no playful cartoon style
- no cluttered streaming-app aesthetic

Color palette:
- charcoal black
- warm off-black
- soft ivory text
- muted teal or muted gold accent
- restrained color use
- high contrast and accessibility-first readability

UI details:
- realistic chord-over-lyrics layout
- segmented controls for view switching
- compact filter chips
- bottom navigation or clean top navigation depending on screen
- large touch-friendly targets
- elegant cards and list rows
- one-handed usability
- realistic music notation density without looking crowded

Composition:
- show 6 to 8 phones in one premium showcase
- front-facing phones with slight perspective
- clean neutral studio background
- focus on the app UI only
- crisp readable interface details
- premium product presentation
- 16:9 aspect ratio
